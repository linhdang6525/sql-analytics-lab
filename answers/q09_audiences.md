## 2. To another analyst

**Finding:** Completed-order revenue declined by 0.25% in H1 2026 compared with H1 2025.

- **Definition:** Revenue is the sum of `order_total` for orders with `status = 'completed'`. Percentage change is calculated as `(H1 2026 revenue − H1 2025 revenue) / H1 2025 revenue × 100`.
- **Filters:** H1 2025 covers 1 January–30 June 2025; H1 2026 covers 1 January–30 June 2026. Only completed orders are included.
- **What would change the number:** Changing the order-status filter or the date boundaries would change the result. The comparison also depends on both periods using the same revenue definition and compatible date coverage.
